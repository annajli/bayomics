3. Key findings

  • Identical structure. All four files share schema AIFI_L3, fg, bg, gene, log2fc, padj, pvalue, stat. The cross-sectional comparisons (Age, Sex, CMV) each have 373,942 rows; Flu Vaccine has 376,678 (every cell-type × gene tested per comparison).
  • Biological Sex shows the strongest, cleanest signal — driven heavily by sex-chromosome genes (e.g. RPS4Y1, XIST, DDX3Y) with extreme log2FC and astronomically small padj, exactly as expected for a male-vs-female contrast.
  • Biological Sex yields the most strong DEGs (315), while Age Group yields the fewest (5) — consistent with a subtler biological effect / lower statistical power.
  • p-value histograms show the anti-conservative spike near 0 for comparisons with real signal, and a flatter uniform shape where effects are weak — a good DESeq2 sanity check.
  • Direction is largely balanced for most contrasts; check the signed bar chart for any foreground/background asymmetry (positive log2FC = higher in the Foreground group).
